# Five-ability research implementation and acceptance

The owner requested all five abilities and remaining real-data evaluation. This change adds the five-ability feature path, reviewed motion sidecar, dynamic conditional phase state, favorite defeat diagnostics, bounded discovery and controlled ablations. It deliberately does not claim a physical rider digital twin or validated real video prediction.

- 1: prior form and agari + identity-linked calibrated reviewed-video metrics. Real video training records not supplied here.
- 2: pre-race load proxies and cumulative phase-state variables. Actual physiological fatigue is not measured; phase training requires authentic labels.
- 3: prior B rate / line leader and competitor interactions, third-line opportunity proxy; existing learned conditional order retained. Real scenario validation required.
- 4: exact favorite loss probability, group-weight sensitivity, conditional sampled losing paths when phase bundle is available. Not causality.
- 5: fixed feature-product pool with data-driven training-only sign consistency and selection on later tune period. No final-period tuning.
- New study runs on same 30,713 archived races. Old and new features and candidate masks are compared at fixed splits. Historical weather/video not invented. The one-shot job emits only aggregate results and verified frozen model parameters in bounded log chunks, never individual records or forecast originals. No production promotion.
- Future 3-entrant comparison remains blocked until independent competitor artifacts and authentic pre-cutoff same-race originals/results arrive. CI cannot substitute for real evidence.
