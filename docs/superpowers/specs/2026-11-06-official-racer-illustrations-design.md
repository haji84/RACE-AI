# Official-profile athlete illustrations

The owner approved the helmet-free Makoto Busujima illustration generated from his official BOAT RACE profile photograph, then instructed us to proceed. Use that natural, faithful illustration style for each athlete; identity, hairstyle and expression come from that athlete's own official profile, not another athlete's illustration.

Integrate illustrations into the existing home, race-list and detail athlete cards. Resolve by sport and stable registration number, and refuse conflicting identity fields or names. Synthetic records must not acquire real people's portraits. Unknown/unavailable illustrations show a neutral preparation state. Failed images fall back without breaking the cards.

Initial batch: 3415 松井繁, 3897 白井英治, 3941 池田浩二, 4238 毒島誠, 4320 峰竜太, 4444 桐生順平. This is an initial batch, not a claim that all 1,645 known athletes already have illustrations. Display batch coverage in settings and make additional entries straightforward.

Keep generated illustrations and official source URLs separate from forecast originals. Serve local assets; viewing a card must not contact the official photo server. Preserve strict CSP, encrypted view files, owner URLs, private keys, forecasts, current workflows and access settings. GitHub Pages remains the deployment target; no new service or paid integration.

Approaches considered: real-time generation adds runtime credentials and cost; embedding official photos does not match the approved illustrated style. Pre-generated, local, registration-bound illustrations fit the existing static site and were selected for implementation. Encode the delivered assets losslessly for the web; retain generated originals.
